# Slice 5: local people store and sign-in (2026-11-07)

Part of #429. The appliance keeps its own people in `accounts/people.json`
(`accounts.rs`: `LocalPerson`, `add/is_local/local_person/local_people/`
`remove_person`); the kernel resolves the config first and the local store
after (`kernel.rs`: `resolve_person`, `person()` delegates); the API signs
local-only people in from the local store with Keycloak live and accepts
their invites (`api.rs`: provider skip, local tail, invite accept, `user/`
policy layer); the shell adds, removes and manages them (`cli.rs`:
`person-add`, `person-remove`, five gates through `managed_accounts`).

Tests: lib 142/142 (new: `local_people_round_trip_and_remove_cleanly`,
`sign_ins_resolve_config_first_then_local`); new `local_people` e2e —
a local person signs in to the API with Keycloak live, a config person
still goes through Keycloak's own flow, a local invite accepts, removal
ends the session and shuts them out. Gates re-run: `app` 17/17,
`admin_local_sign_in` 1/1, `admin_sign_in` 9/9. Clippy: no new issues
(77 before and after; the one rustc warning is `proactive.rs`, #425's
in-progress work).

Notes: the e2e needed a rebuilt `magnus-appliance` binary
(`cargo build -p magnus-invariants --bins`) — `cargo test -p
magnus-scratch` alone runs the stale one. Next: slice 6, the console
