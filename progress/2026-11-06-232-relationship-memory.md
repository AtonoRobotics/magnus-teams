Relationship memory is built (behavior step 3, first part; #232): Magnus records how a person wants it to work with them with `relationship_note`, keeps it in their own bundle document `/users/<person>/relationship.md`, and every run and chat turn of theirs carries it in its system context, with its version logged per model call. The person sees, corrects and removes their notes in Settings, Data and privacy, or by telling Magnus; no other person's run, the owner or an admin sees them, and erasure removes them. Each run also sees Magnus's recent untainted messages to the person from their other conversations, up to 16KB; reactions and disagreement records (the rest of step 3) are next.
