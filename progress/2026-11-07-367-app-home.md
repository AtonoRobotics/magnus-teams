# App home feed (#367)

Opening the app now shows what changed since the last session. New
`POST /v1/home/opened` feed endpoint (ended/decided/arrived plus
queued observations, recording the open) and `POST /v1/inbox/opened`
for presented items (`Ev::HomeOpened`, `Ev::ItemOpened`, opened and
resolution timestamps in state, `opened_at` on inbox renders). The
desktop fetches the feed at boot, opens the needs panel beside home
when there is news, and reports presented observations.

- `crates/magnus/src/events.rs`, `state.rs`, `api.rs`, `inbox.rs`:
  endpoints, events, state maps, render field.
- `desktop/ui/app.js`: boot fetch, feed section, opened reporting.
- `tests/scratch/tests/home.rs`: feed contents across three opens,
  opened recorded on presentation, dismissal landing in decided.

Verified: new test fails before (404) and passes after; `node
--check` on the UI. Desktop webdriver coverage left to CI.
