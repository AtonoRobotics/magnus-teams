`migrate` now refuses a kernel role that can switch with SET ROLE, directly or through other roles, to one that can change the log or holds superuser-like rights, closing the NOINHERIT route of #186. A new Postgres regression in `tests/scratch/tests/log_review.rs` makes the role three ways (direct `pg_write_all_data`, through a role in between, a superuser role), shows each route is real, and checks that once the membership is taken away no role the kernel can switch to can change, delete or empty the log.
