Task proposal cards are built (behavior step 4, second part; #254). In chat Magnus proposes one task with `propose_task`: a card with what it noticed and where, the outcome, why now, and what the task needs (permissions, an estimated cost, any input from the person), resting on evidence checked as a suggestion's is. The person approves it (the task starts for them and can spend at most the estimate, shown on the task) or dismisses it with a reason; it is not put off. Proposals and their answers are in the suggestion ledger, and a dismissed one comes back only with what changed. "Handle things like this" moves to step 5 (standing authority, #235).
