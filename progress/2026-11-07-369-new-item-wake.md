# New-item wakes, digest v1 (#369)

Watched reads (`Watch` on the connector config) diff their answers
against per-person baselines; new item ids wake a review naming the
count, first sighting seeds silently, and the baseline advances
only when the review starts.

- `config.rs`, `catalog.rs`, `connectors.rs`: `Watch` config.
- `events.rs`, `state.rs`: `WatchedSeen`, `watched_seen` baselines.
- `proactive.rs`: `note_watched`, `review_wake_items`, boolean
  wake returns, `watched_ids` plus unit tests.
- `boundary.rs`: hook after watched answers.
- `tests/scratch/tests/new_items.rs`: seed silent, then "2 new in
  mail" wakes a review; `Spec::watch` builder.

Verified: integration test fails before (60s timeout, no wake) and
passes after; lib 116/116; proactive 9/9; loop_policy 5/5;
reviewer 6/6; home 1/1. Scheduled polling filed as #406.
