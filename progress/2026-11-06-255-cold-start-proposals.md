Proposals at cold start and for each newly connected app are built (behavior step 4, last part; #255). At a person's first sign-in with nothing here yet, and when they connect an app for the first time, Magnus opens a conversation itself ("What I could take over", or "... in <App>"): it looks at what it may see (the app through its tools; at cold start their apps, the company's knowledge and their recent work) and puts two or three suggestions or task proposals citing what it read, answered like any card, then a short message in its own voice. Each starts once ever, never on a reconnection, and not while the kill switch is on. The instruction it runs on is the appliance's (`instructions/opener.md`, `opener-app.md`) and is never shown or transcribed as the person's message. The empty Tasks and Schedules list the cards still waiting, linked to their conversation. The setting `[proposals] openers` (on by default) turns it off; the test harness's default sets it off (one line in `tests/invariants/src/appliance.rs`, approved by the owner on 2026-11-06), and the tests for openers turn it on. Step 4 is done; step 5 (standing authority) is next.
