Automation suggestion cards are built (behavior step 4, first part; #253). In chat Magnus suggests something it could take over with `suggest`: a card with its title, plan, the evidence it rests on (a tool result the turn read, or a task of the person's, checked to be real or the card is refused) and what it may read, change or send. The person accepts it (a task starts, or with timing a schedule), says Not now, or declines it with a reason. Schedule cards carry the same plan, permissions and evidence and take the same answers. Every card and its answer are in the suggestion ledger, which every later run of the person reads in its context; a declined one is suggested again only with what changed. The task proposal card and the cold-start proposals are next in step 4.
