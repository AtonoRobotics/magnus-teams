Standing authority is built (behavior step 5; #235). A person grants Magnus standing authority over a kind of their own work in two ways only: "Handle things like this" on a task proposal (the grant is set from a form prefilled from the proposal, and its task starts under it), or accepting a grant card Magnus puts with `propose_grant` when they ask in conversation. The kernel writes each grant as a Cedar policy from its fields (the apps it covers; sends and what cannot be undone excluded unless lifted; spending money always excluded) with a budget of model cost per rolling day, week or month. In a task started under a grant, what the organisation's policy would ask about goes ahead within it, for actions the person could approve themselves; the Rule of Two and the audience rule still ask, and every covered action is a `grant_used` event listed under the grant. Grants are listed in Settings (Data and privacy) with their spend, actions and policy, editable and revocable there or with `revoke_grant` in conversation, and Magnus sees the live ones in each conversation. Invariant 6 is reworded to "autonomy comes from policy: the owner's for the organisation, each person's own grants over their own work within it", with a test added.
