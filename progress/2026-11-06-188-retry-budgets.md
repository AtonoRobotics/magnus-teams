Before every model request, retries included, the agent loop now reads the run as it is now and stops without asking when its dollars or model calls are spent, it was cancelled or suspended, the kill switch is on, or an update is under way (#188). The stops are the loop's and the boundary budget stage's own, so a dollar stop resumes with "extend" like any budget stop. Tests in `tests/scratch/tests/model_retries.rs` cover a charged failure past `max_usd` and a cancel during the retry wait.
