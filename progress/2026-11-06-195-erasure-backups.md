Deleting a person while a backup cannot be rewritten is now reported incomplete, in the erase answer, the console, the owner's Backups and the audit, and the pending cleanup is in the log, retried at start and hourly until every backup is rewritten (#195). A restore leaves out content the removals named, from a record kept in the state directory and beside the backups, so a backup left holding an erased person's content does not bring it back. The two scratch tests in `tests/scratch/tests/person_erasure.rs` cover both against a real Keycloak. A backups directory gone or unreadable keeps the erasure pending, and the record of removals is only added to, so a restore of an older backup does not forget a later erasure (review of #243).
