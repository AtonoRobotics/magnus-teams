The commitment ledger is built (behavior step 6; #236). Magnus records what the person and it said they would do with `record_commitment`, in chat and in tasks, and the kernel records it only when its quote is found verbatim in the person's own words of the run (or Magnus's own replies, for its commitments) or in the result of an app read the call names; a duplicate still open is refused. Each commitment has who, what, for whom, by when, where it was said and its status (open, done, released); overdue is derived from the date. Every run of the person carries their open commitments in its context as they stood when it started, and their taint, so a commitment read in untrusted mail taints the runs that carry it. Magnus marks one done or released with `update_commitment`; the person sees, corrects, completes, releases and removes theirs in Settings ("What you and Magnus said you'd do") through `/v1/commitments`, which no one else can read. Raising overdue ones is the proactive loop's (step 7, #237), which is next.
