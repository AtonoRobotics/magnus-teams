Deleting a person now removes the approvals given to them, in the state reducer, so an owner's standing or window approval of their actions no longer covers a new account later bound to the same person ID, live or after a restart (#191). Approvals the deleted person gave to others stay, governed as before by the gate's check that their approver may still give them. The scratch test in `tests/scratch/tests/person_erasure.rs` covers both against a real Keycloak.
