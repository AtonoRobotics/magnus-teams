# A person's own knowledge and rules stay theirs

The owner, 2026-11-11: "users should be isolated from each other securely unless they choose to shares something in teams. Then only what they share should be shared". Spec rev 145: "Personal memory, taste memory and personal files never appear in a team thread"; "Data crosses from personal to team only by an explicit share".

- **The owner could delete an entry only its person reads.** The trash let the owner move any knowledge entry to their own trash (its title with it) and delete it for good, including one whose readers were that person alone. Now the owner moderates entries shared with groups, as before, and an entry only some people read is theirs (`Projection::may_trash`).
- **What an agent filed from a person's own account was readable by a whole group.** A run that read private content from an app filed what it learned for the app's access groups. For an app each person signs in to with their own account (and one a person built), the groups say who may use the app, not who may read that person's mailbox, so the entry is now filed for the person alone, and reaches others only when they share it (`knowledge::app_readers`). An app on the organisation's key, one store for all its users, still files for its groups.
- **A person's agent could propose a change to someone else's own rules.** `propose_policy` took any `user/<id>` layer, putting the run's words and rationale in that other person's inbox. Now a run proposes to the organisation's layer, a group's, or its own person's only (`policy::proposable`).
