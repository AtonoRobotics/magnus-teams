# A workspace's owners and admins moderate its channels

The owner, 2026-11-11: "The teams admin can moderate and delete it only from teams." Spec rev 145 gives an owner or admin the deletion of any file on the team volume; it says nothing of messages, and the 2026-11-11 decision on deleting messages let only a message's sender delete it.

Now a workspace's owners and admins also delete any message in its channels, by the same route and in the same way (gone for everyone, its text out of the log and the backups). A direct conversation is its people's alone, so nobody moderates it, admin or not. A message lives only in the team, so deleting it takes nothing from its sender's own space. The app shows Delete on others' channel messages to a workspace's owners and admins.
