# Local console posts are origin-bound (2026-11-07)

- The local sign-in and step-up POSTs answer only submissions from the console's own pages: exact Origin match, else a Referer on the host, refusing when both are absent (#456). Reason: without it a malicious site signs a visiting admin into the attacker's console session (login CSRF); every browser that submits a cross-origin form sends Origin, so strictness costs nothing.
- The check runs before credentials. Reason: a forged post must neither sign in nor burn the account's lockout — otherwise the endpoint doubles as a lockout cannon.
