# Automation suggestion cards, the evidence check and the suggestion ledger (2026-11-06)

- A chat turn suggests something Magnus could do for the person with one tool, `suggest` (app `chat`, a read like `create_schedule`, chat turns only): title, plan, evidence, permissions, and optionally the timing, end and budget a schedule takes (checked as `create_schedule` checks them). With timing it is a schedule; without, one task. Nothing happens until the person answers the card in the thread. Reason: behavior spec (an automation suggestion: title, plan, evidence, permissions; Accept creates the task or schedule). Test: `suggestion_cards.rs` `a_suggestion_citing_what_it_read_becomes_a_card_and_accepting_it_starts_a_task_or_a_schedule`.
- The evidence is refused, and the card with it, unless each item points at something real: `call`, a tool call of this turn whose result was recorded and was not an error, of a tool that reads (not a card, `suggest`, `create_schedule`, `offer_app`, nor a run record such as `show`); or `task`, a task of this person's not in the trash. The refusal names the item and says a retry with it will not help. The card keeps each item resolved (the tool's name, or the task's title), so the person sees what it rests on. Reason: behavior spec ("the validator refuses a card whose evidence does not point at something real"); a result this turn read is the "item Magnus actually read, recorded when read" (its intent and result are in the log), and a task is an event in the log. Test: `evidence_that_points_at_nothing_real_is_refused` (an unknown call id, a card's call, another person's task; her own task passes).
- A schedule card is a suggestion too: `create_schedule`'s card also carries its plan (its text), permissions (its scope) and evidence (the turn's own request, "you asked for it"); its kind, title, button and fields stay. Test: `a_schedule_card_is_a_suggestion_put_off_or_declined_with_a_reason`.
- The person answers a card with accept (or confirm, as before), later ("Not now"), or decline, which needs a reason (400 without one). Only the person the card was put to answers it (404 for anyone else), once (409). Accepting a suggestion without timing starts a task as `create_task` does from that turn (for the person, with the turn's taint, model, files and project, posting its result to the thread), once, with the card's budget and its permissions as its scope; with timing it makes the schedule as a confirmed schedule card does. Events: `card_declined` gains `person` and `reason` (both defaulted, so earlier logs still load), new `card_deferred` and `card_accepted` (the task it started). Reason: behavior spec (Accept, Not now, Decline with a reason). Test: the first and third tests.
- A decline's reason is the person's words, so it is content (`events::CONTENT`): in the content store under their wall, erased with them, and removed with the conversation it was given in when that leaves the trash. Test: `a_decline_needs_a_reason_which_later_runs_read_in_the_ledger_also_after_a_restart` (the reason is not in the log's record); `suggestions::tests::the_ledger_is_the_cards_and_answers_before_the_run` (a removed conversation's holding takes its cards' reasons; erasure takes the answers).
- The suggestion ledger is every card of the person's (schedules and suggestions) with its answer, from the projection: the cards in their chat turns' results and the answers in `cards`. Every run of theirs carries it in its system context after the reactions, under "Your suggestions to this person and their answers", as it stood when the run started (cards proposed and answers given before then, C1), newest first within the same budget as the recent messages (`relationship::recent_lines`), shown oldest first, each with its card id, and saying a declined one comes back only with `again` and `changed`. A card deleted from its thread, or in a conversation in the trash, is not in it, as for the reactions; a card from a run that read untrusted or private content is named without its words. Reason: behavior spec ("every card and its answer go in the suggestion ledger, which the model reads before it suggests again"); #233's limits for the same context. Test: the third test (live and after a restart, not in another person's run); `suggestions::tests::the_ledger_is_the_cards_and_answers_before_the_run`.
- A declined card comes back only with what changed: `suggest` with `again` naming a declined card of the person's needs `changed`; a title matching a declined card of theirs (trimmed, any case) without `again` is refused, naming that card's id. The card shows what changed. Reason: behavior spec ("a declined one comes back only when circumstances changed, saying what changed"); the title catches the same suggestion made again without saying so. Test: `a_declined_suggestion_comes_back_only_with_what_changed`. A declined title comes back only naming that card in `again`, whatever else `again` names (review of #256). Test: `a_declined_title_does_not_come_back_by_naming_another_card`.
- Timing in a `suggest` is a schedule's whenever any of `every_minutes`, `daily_at`, `until`, `max_runs` is given, and is checked as `create_schedule` checks it: a negative interval or an end with no rule is refused, never read as a one-off task. Only 0 or "" count as not given, as in `schedule_rule`, since a model that must fill every field sends them for one it does not mean. Reason: review of #256. Test: `timing_given_is_checked_as_a_schedules`.
- The task proposal (noticed, outcome, why now, needs; Approve, Dismiss, Handle things like this) and the cold-start proposals are the rest of step 4, not in this change.
