# Task proposal cards (2026-11-06)

- A chat turn proposes one task with one tool, `propose_task` (app `chat`, a read like `suggest`, chat turns only): title, text (what the task does, complete enough to work from alone), noticed (what and where: which app, thread or task), outcome, why now, needs (permissions, an estimated cost in dollars, and optionally what it needs from the person), and evidence. Every text field is required and not blank, except the input it needs. Nothing starts until the person approves the card in the thread. Reason: behavior spec (a task proposal: noticed, outcome, why now, needs; Approve starts the task). Test: `task_proposals.rs` `a_proposal_citing_what_it_read_becomes_a_card_and_approving_it_starts_one_task_within_the_estimate`.
- The estimate must be above 0 and at most the appliance's budget `max_usd` (one task's limit), or the card is refused ("a retry with the same estimate will not help"). The card's budget is the appliance's with `max_usd` set to the estimate, so the approved task cannot spend more than the cost the person approved; its scope is the permissions joined with "; ". The task's detail (`GET /v1/tasks/{id}`) now shows its run's budget. Reason: the person approves a cost, and the existing per-run budget is what enforces one. Test: the first test (the task's `budget.max_usd` is the estimate); `a_proposal_missing_a_field_or_with_a_bad_estimate_or_evidence_is_refused` (0 and $51 against the $50 limit).
- The evidence is a suggestion's and checked by the same code (`Kernel::grounded`, shared with `suggest`): each item a tool result this turn read or a task of the person's, one bad item refusing the card. So is the rule that what the person declined comes back only with `again` naming that card and `changed`; a dismissed proposal is a declined card, and a title matching any declined card of theirs, suggestion or proposal, needs both. A proposal's own result is a card, not evidence. Test: the second test (an unknown call); `a_dismissed_proposal_comes_back_only_with_what_changed`.
- The person approves (`approve`, or `accept`/`confirm`) or dismisses (`dismiss`, or `decline`) the card, as any card: only the person it was put to (404 for anyone else), once (409). Approving starts the task exactly as accepting an untimed suggestion does (`task_from_turn`: for the person, with the turn's taint, model, files and project, posting its result to the thread; `card_accepted`). Dismissing needs a reason (400 without one) and is recorded as `card_declined` with it, content as a decline's is. "Not now" is not offered for a proposal: `later` is refused with 400 naming approve and dismiss, since the spec gives a proposal only Approve and Dismiss and something noticed now that is put off has usually gone stale. The thread entry is `proposal_card` (state open, confirmed or declined, with the task it started or the reason); the next turn reads it as "Proposed the task …" with its answer. Test: the first test and `a_dismissal_needs_a_reason_which_later_runs_read_in_the_ledger_and_a_proposal_is_not_put_off`.
- Proposals are in the suggestion ledger with the other cards, as "you proposed the task" with its text and outcome, answered "they approved it" or "they dismissed it, saying …"; the section's text names task proposals and dismissals. A proposal from a run that read untrusted or private content is named without its words, as any card. The section's heading is unchanged. Test: the third test.
- "Handle things like this" is not on the card: it opens a standing-authority grant, which is behavior step 5 (#235) and not built; the button moves there and is built with grants. Approving a proposal never creates a grant (spec, Standing authority).
- The cold-start proposals are the rest of step 4, not in this change.
