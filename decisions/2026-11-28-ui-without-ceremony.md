# The UI without ceremony or theater

The owner, 2026-11-28: "review computer and UI redesign the UI to remove
ceremony, and theater."

Ceremony is anything said twice, or explained when the screen already shows
it. Theater is anything that performs instead of informing: made-up progress,
demos of states that are not happening, showcase tiles and decoration that
moves. Each goes; what the spec asks for stays, said once.

## The computer

- The strip above the screen is the one place that says how the computer
  stands. Its pill carries who holds control and whether the agent is paused
  ("You are in control · Magnus paused"), as the spec's Preview asks, and a
  short line adds only what the pill cannot hold: for whom and on what the
  agent works, the queue position, what a takeover request is for.
- Gone: the sentence after the pill that restated it ("Magnus has control.
  It is working…"), the caption along the bottom of the screen that said it a
  third time, and the "You're in control" overlay with its second I'm done,
  which covered the screen the person was driving. The frame's tooltip still
  says a click takes control.
- The wake shows its real progress: the phase in words and "step 2 of 3".
  The bar with invented shares (20, 50, 80 percent) is gone; the spec asks
  for the progress, and the phases are all there is to show.
- The paragraphs that explained the page ("It is always there…", how apps
  are recorded) are gone.

## The rest of the app

- A new conversation is its title and the composer. The "Autonomous
  launchpads" with their emoji, the prismatic showcase tiles (a colour
  mosaic, "Editorial Playbooks", a "Live Workspace" badge) and the cobalt
  halo behind them are gone. They were sample work, not the person's.
- Settings → Appearance picks a companion without the "Live Companion
  Preview" that acted out states (Listening, Thinking, Done…) nothing was in.
- Apps & Skills opens on its search and catalog, without the Featured banner
  that advertised one app of the catalog.
- The top bar says where the person is and the month's spend, and holds an
  admin's kill switch. The companion picker and Light / Dark leave it: they
  are set once, in Settings, not offered on every screen.
- The animated background (Particles) is off by default. Ribbons,
  Constellation and Particles stay in Settings for whoever chooses them. A
  saved look holds the whole record, so one saved before look 3 holds
  Particles as the default it was then: it moves to Off once, keeping the
  rest, as the move to Nova did (2026-11-26-saved-looks-move-to-nova-once.md).
  A background chosen after that stays.
- Kept, because they inform or act: Task Replay, keyboard approval triage,
  the companion itself, the wake's progress, the kill switch.

## Two more, from the installed app on test.427

- The line above the composer speaks for its own conversation. It read the
  whole app's state, so with work running anywhere it said "Working across
  your apps…" under a conversation whose answer ("pong", answered in 3s)
  was already in. Now it follows that conversation's own answers, cards and
  asks, plus the person's own states (listening, holding the computer); its
  colour and visibility key on the line's own state. "Working across your
  apps…" became "Working…": the task card says what the work is.
- What's new drops the commit's whole trailer block, not only "-by" lines:
  "Claude-Session: https://claude.ai/code/session_…" is the team's note,
  not news for the person.
