# Message reactions and the disagreement log (2026-11-06)

- Each of Magnus's messages in a person's thread (an answer, what it said when it could not answer, a task's result) takes a reaction in the app, "that was great" (`great`) or "that was annoying" (`annoying`), through `POST /v1/chat/{thread}/entries/{entry}/reaction`; the same call with `null` takes it back, and the latest counts. Each is an event, `reacted`, naming the person, thread and message, never its words, so it holds no content and the state rebuilds it on replay. The person's own messages and other cards take none. Reason: behavior spec ("Any message can get a reaction, logged"); a reaction is a click from a fixed pair, like a verdict, and the message's words already sit in the store under its run. Test: `reactions_disagreements.rs` `a_reaction_is_logged_changed_and_taken_back_and_every_later_run_sees_it_also_after_a_restart`.
- A reaction is separate from the verdict (right, partly, wrong). Reason: the verdict grades whether the outcome was right (DECISIONS, 2026-10-29); a reaction says how the message landed with the person, which is what relationship memory is for.
- The reaction shows on the message only in the person's own view of their thread (`GET /v1/chat/{thread}`), not in the thread as `thread_entries` gives it to anyone else (an admin's read of their chats). Another person cannot react to their message (their thread is not found). Test: `reactions_and_disagreements_are_seen_by_no_one_else`.
- Every run and chat turn of the person carries their reactions in its system context after the relationship memory and recent messages, under "How this person reacted to your messages": each with the words of the message it is on, newest first within the same 16KB budget as the recent messages (`relationship::BUDGET`), shown oldest first. Only reactions given before the run started, so its context stays the same at every call (C1); only those on messages their thread still shows (a deleted message, or one in the trash, drops out, as its words left what the model reads); a message from a run that read untrusted or private content is named without its words, which would carry its taint into every later run. Reason: behavior spec ("Magnus sees reactions with its relationship memory"), and #232's limits for the same context. Test: `a_reaction_is_logged_changed_and_taken_back_...` (live, after a change, in a task, after a restart, and gone once taken back); `reactions::tests::reactions_and_open_disagreements_are_those_before_the_run`.
- Magnus logs a disagreement with one dedicated tool, `disagreement`, in app `memory` like `relationship_note` (so a chat turn may use it, and the memory pass may not): `record` (position: what it advised; decision: what the person decided instead) and later `outcome` (its id and what happened), once; the first outcome stands. Events `disagreement_recorded` and `disagreement_outcome`, their words in the content store, walled to the person and erased with them. Reason: behavior spec ("Disagreements are logged: Magnus's position, the person's decision, and later the outcome"); the model is the one that knows it advised otherwise. Test: `a_disagreement_is_logged_with_its_outcome_and_later_runs_see_it_while_open_also_after_a_restart` (the record's words are not in the log's row).
- Every run of the person carries the disagreements still open when it started, with their ids, under "Disagreements with this person still open", within the same budget; one with an outcome leaves the context. The person reads their whole log, open and settled, at `GET /v1/disagreements` and in Settings, Data and privacy ("Where you decided otherwise"); no one else's, whoever asks. Reason: the outcome is recorded later, so the open ones must be readable back in a later run; the person sees what is kept about them, as for their notes. Test: the same.
- `disagreement` refuses a run that read untrusted or private content, as `relationship_note` does (#232). Reason: what it records enters every later run of the person. Test: `a_run_that_read_outside_content_records_no_disagreement`.
- Erasing a person removes their reactions and disagreements from the state, and the disagreements' words from the store with the rest of their content. Test: `reactions::tests::reactions_and_open_disagreements_are_those_before_the_run` (the erasure event).
- The state keeps every reaction given to a message, with when (a taking back as none), not only the latest, and a run takes each as it stood when the run started. Reason: review of #252 (a reaction changed or taken back mid-run changed the run's context at its next model call, against C1). Test: `reactions::tests::reactions_and_open_disagreements_are_those_before_the_run` (changed after the start, it counts as it was then).
- A reaction on a task's result drops out of the context once the answer that started the task is deleted, as the thread hides the result with it. Reason: review of #252 (the result's words came back into every run after the person deleted the answer). Test: the same (without the check, the deleted answer's result was still carried).
