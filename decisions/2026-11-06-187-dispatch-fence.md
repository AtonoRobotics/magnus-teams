# A cancelled task's call does not go out (2026-11-06)

- An app's call goes out only while its run, read live at that moment, is still running: the boundary checks it right before a remote MCP call and before a brokered call, and again inside the brokered fence once the call has its microVM (beside the stops, the kill switch and the sign-in). Reason: the gate works from a copy of the run taken before it waits on the pin, the destination read and the sign-in, and a cancel or suspension that landed during those waits still let the call go out (#187). The same idea as `may_ask_model` for model requests. Tests: `draft_lock.rs` `a_send_whose_task_is_cancelled_while_the_gate_reads_the_draft_sends_nothing` (remote MCP; fails on master with the send effective once) and `gmail.rs` `an_approved_send_whose_task_is_cancelled_while_the_gate_reads_the_draft_sends_nothing` (brokered; fails on master with `POST /drafts/send` reaching Gmail).
- A call held back this way is recorded as failed, "was not sent", except a resend of a step whose earlier attempt has no result: that is recorded as unknown, saying the earlier attempt may have reached the app. Reason: a write that may have happened is never reported as not sent. Test: `draft_lock.rs` `a_resend_held_back_for_a_cancelled_task_is_reported_as_unknown` (fails on master with the send dispatched a second time).
- The brokered test pauses the gate's draft read with a hold on Gmail's stand-in (`StandInGmail::hold_draft_read`, test code only); the remote MCP tests use `FakeMcp::hold`. Reason: the pause sits at the provider, so the product has no test hook.
