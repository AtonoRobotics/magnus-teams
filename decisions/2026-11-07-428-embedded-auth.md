# Embedded auth replaces Keycloak (2026-11-07)

- Magnus signs people in itself: local users, passwords+TOTP, invite links, one login, one panel (owner's decision, #428; spec `docs/proposals/embedded-auth.md`). Reason: the realm served only Magnus, and every pain (provisioning, realm SMTP, subject rebinding, tunnel exposure) came from keeping a second user database; a lighter IdP keeps the split. Non-goals: open signup, federation now, passkeys (ruling stands). The `Identity` trait stays the seam for enterprise federation later.
- The Keycloak direction stops now: #290, #292 and #356 closed as superseded; the live hand-built realm stays until the #429 cutover. Salvaged from #292: `config/keycloak-client.py` takes `--admin-url`, `--admin-file` and `--ca` as required, since the retired deployment's defaults exist nowhere and the only caller already passes them. Test: a bare run exits 2 naming the args (fails before with the dead-CA `FileNotFoundError`).
- `release/admin-on-appliance.sh` stops pointing at `release/identity-on-appliance.sh`, which will never exist: the missing-identity errors say the live identity is configured by hand until #429. Test: the #312 assertion on `status_admin_ca` passes unchanged.
