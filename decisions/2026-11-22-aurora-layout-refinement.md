# Aurora layout, choreography, canvas, and 3-island Teams refinement

## Choice
Refine Aurora and Obsidian 2 Aurora spatial composition, header clarity, canvas ergonomics, streaming polish, and Teams layout (without changing the Aurora / Obsidian 2 Aurora frosted-glass palette or single `--accent` model, and without altering non-Aurora styles):
1. **Calm centered new conversation (1A):** Center `.hello-hero` and `.hello-head` vertically and horizontally in both `aurora` and `obsidian-aurora`, hide the dense playbook dashboard chrome inside `.hello-hero` (`.hello-caps`, `.hero-tabs`, `.hello-cards`, `.hello-pillars` — full playbooks remain in Explore), and show 4 compact starter chips (`.aurora-hello-chips`) that populate and focus the composer on click.
2. **Slim conversation header (2A):** Keep Approvals (with count badge) directly in `.stage-dock` and fold Tasks, Routines, Rules, Files, and Apps into one compact glass popover menu (`.stage-dock-more`) with item-click, outside-click, and Escape dismissal.
3. **Content-adaptive canvas width (3A):** Mark `.stage-artifact` with `data-canvas-size="compact"` (380–460px for approvals/decisions and lists) or `data-canvas-size="wide"` (~54% up to 820px for Cloud Computer, tasks, files, routines), while honoring manual `#workspace-splitter` drags/keyboard resizes (`body[data-splitter-custom]`, double-click resets).
4. **`Inspect Canvas →` footer row (4A):** Keep the 15s auto-dismiss on non-urgent bento tiles and append a quiet `.aurora-canvas-row` footer (`Inspect Canvas →`) on completed answers backed by a finished task or rich artifact so users can reopen work on the canvas in one click.
5. **Read-only decision preview (5A):** Parse high-signal human fields (To/Recipients, Subject/Title, Channel/URL/Amount, Body/Message) from `.ask-content .what .field` into a clean `.decision-preview` card directly above `<details class="ask-content">`, keeping every raw audit field intact inside the disclosure.
6. **Sequential multi-block streaming with DOM unwrap:** Thread the word cursor across `.answer-body > *` blocks so multi-paragraph answers stream sequentially top-to-bottom, then unwrap temporary `.stream-word` spans back to plain text nodes after animation completes.
7. **True 3-island Teams layout (6A):** Hide the redundant outer `.shell::before` frame on `body[data-team]`, remove `.team-shell`'s inner `10px` padding so outer padding matches the `12px` shell inset uniformly, give `.team-center` matching frosted glass, animate `.team-shell` `grid-template-columns` smoothly when toggling `.work-closed`, and mount live `AuroraAvatar` orbs into `.team-rail-head .room-orb` and `.room-video-listening .room-orb`.

## Reason
Compared against the bundled Obsidian 2 agent demo, Aurora's main gaps were layout density and spatial choreography rather than palette: a heavy dashboard empty state, a 6-icon header dock competing with thread controls, a fixed 49% canvas width even for small approval cards, lost one-click canvas re-entry after bento auto-dismiss, raw key-value disclosures on decisions, simultaneous paragraph word streaming, and a double-framed Teams workspace whose Work Canvas snapped on toggle. Each choice follows the owner's approved plan while keeping non-Aurora themes and backend contracts untouched.
