# An opener conversation deleted (#275, review of #274)

- The appliance keeps which conversations Magnus opened, by person and task (`State::openers`, from the `task_created` event's `chat.opener`, which a deletion does not erase), and an opener that started once never starts again, even when its conversation was deleted or removed for good. Reason: the trash takes a conversation's turns out of `tasks`, so the opener's own check (its derived task exists) and the cold-start check (no task, no branch) both passed again, and a deleted opener ran again at the next sign-in, costing model calls and reading apps, its new conversation hidden. Erasing the person removes their record with the rest of what is theirs. Test: `cold_start_proposals.rs` `a_deleted_opener_conversation_is_listed_by_its_title_and_does_not_open_again`.
- A whole conversation Magnus opened is listed in the trash by its title ("What I could take over", or "... in <App>"), as a single opener entry already was, not by its first message, which is the appliance's instruction. Test: the same.
