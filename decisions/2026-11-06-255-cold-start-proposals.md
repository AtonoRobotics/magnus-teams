# Proposals at cold start and for each newly connected app (2026-11-06)

- Magnus opens a conversation itself, with one chat turn of its own (an opener), at two moments: the person's cold start, a sign-in (`signed_in`) while they have no conversation or task here at all; and their first connection of an app (`app_connected`), never a reconnection of one they connected before (the state counts each person's connections of each app, from the log, so connections from before this change count too). The turn is started in the background after the sign-in or connection is written, so neither waits for the model. Each opener has ids derived from the person (and the app), so it starts once ever: a second sign-in, a restart or a retry finds it; one lock lets only one start at a time. Reason: behavior spec ("at cold start, and for each newly connected app, Magnus looks at what it may see and proposes two or three things it could take over"); a chat turn is where cards live, so its cards are answered, checked and put in the ledger by the code that does so for every card (`decide_card`, `Kernel::grounded`, the suggestion ledger), unchanged. Test: `cold_start_proposals.rs` `a_first_sign_in_opens_one_conversation_whose_cards_cite_what_it_read_and_are_answered_like_any`, `connecting_an_app_the_first_time_opens_one_conversation_that_reads_it_and_reconnecting_does_not`.
- A cold start skipped under the kill switch is not lost: nothing starts while the switch is on (checked before starting, and `create_run` refuses as for any new work), and the person's next sign-in after its release is still their cold start while they have nothing here. Reason: owner's ruling 11 (new work is refused under the switch, not queued); the person should not miss their first proposals because of when they signed in. Test: `nothing_opens_while_the_kill_switch_is_on`.
- The opener's text is the appliance's instruction, in the versioned instructions (`instructions/opener.md`, and `opener-app.md` with the app's name): look at what you may see (the app through its tools; at cold start the connected apps, the company's knowledge and the person's recent work), put two or three things you could take over with `suggest` or `propose_task`, each citing what you read, then a short message in your own voice. The chat instructions version includes both, so a turn's logged version names what it opened with. It is the turn's task text in the log (so the run replays as it ran) with no taint of its own; the run takes the taint of what it reads, as any turn. Reason: issue #255; instruction wording belongs with the other instructions, not in Rust.
- The turn is marked an opener (`opener` on its chat origin: the app's name and title, none at cold start; absent in older logs, which load as before). The thread shows it as Magnus's opening (entry `opener`, naming the app), never as a message of the person's; a later turn reads it as "you opened this conversation yourself", not as what they said; a task started from one of its cards is not handed the instruction as their request; neither the opening nor its answer can be sent again as their message (400); in the trash it is titled as the conversation. The conversation is titled "What I could take over", or "... in <App>". Reason: the instruction is not the person's words, and nothing may show them words they did not say. Test: the first test (no `message` entry and no instruction in the thread, her reply's request, the accepted card's task, resend refused).
- The empty Tasks and Schedules show the cards openers put that still wait on an answer (title, linked to their conversation), introduced in Magnus's voice ("Nothing here yet. I had a look around, and these are mine to take off your hands as soon as you say so:"); with none, the lines as before. They come from the conversation list the app already polls (`GET /v1/chat`): a conversation Magnus opened carries `opener` and `open_cards` (a card answered, put off, or deleted from the thread leaves it). Reason: spec review on #255 (empty states should show what the person could hand off, in the character's voice); no new endpoint. The character reaches the app only through what Magnus says (DECISIONS.md, "Identity and characters": no picker, no setting), so the line is written in its voice. Test: the first test (both cards listed, one dropped as each is answered, none for another person).
- Openers are an appliance setting, `[proposals] openers`, on unless set to false (`config.rs`, `Proposals`). Reason: with openers on, existing tests that sign people in or connect apps with scripted models run an extra model turn, and two fail: `connectors.rs` `the_owner_adds_an_app_each_person_signs_in_with_their_own_account_and_the_kill_switch_revokes_every_sign_in` (the app's `list_events` is called twice, once by carol's opener) and `person_erasure.rs` `a_person_deleted_takes_the_approvals_given_to_them_but_not_those_they_gave` (no approval item: the opener took the scripted step). With openers off the same 25 suites (119 tests, the new ones turning openers on) pass. The test harness's default config is to set it off (`"proposals": { "openers": false }` in `tests/invariants/src/appliance.rs`), with the new tests turning it on (`reconfigure`); that file is under `tests/invariants/`, which changes only with the owner's approval; the owner approved this one line in the session (2026-11-06, "Approve the harness"), and it is applied here.
