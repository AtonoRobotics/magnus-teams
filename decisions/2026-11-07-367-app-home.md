# Home feed and opened-tracking (#367)

Split from #237: opening the app shows what changed since the
person's last session, and presented observations record opened.

Two endpoints. `POST /v1/home/opened` returns the feed — ended tasks
(completed/failed, no reviews or chat turns), decided cards, new
arrivals, and queued observations, newest first — and records the
open (`Ev::HomeOpened`), so the next open starts there. A first open
looks back a day. `POST /v1/inbox/opened {ids}` records each visible
item opened (`Ev::ItemOpened`); unknown or another person's ids are
ignored, so a stale app never fails the open.

Three state maps, following `last_sign_in`: `home_last_open` per
person, `items_opened` and `items_resolved_at` per item. Opened is
first-presentation-wins (`or_insert`): the app reports presented
items on every open, and opened means first seen, for response-time.
Inbox renders carry `opened_at`. A change landing between the feed
read and the open event is marked seen without being shown; the
window is milliseconds, the safe direction on failure is re-showing,
and that trade is accepted.

The desktop fetches the feed once at boot; with news, and landing on
home, the needs panel opens beside the conversation with a "Since
you were last here" section above the action lists. The panel reports
the observation and interrupt ids it renders. Chat-thread cards are
another surface and do not report.

Tests: `home.rs` `home_shows_what_changed_and_tracks_opened` (fails
without: 404 on the new routes). No desktop webdriver test: the
contract is covered server-side and the rendering reuses the existing
card and chip patterns; the change rides CI's desktop job.
