# Right Companion Panel Sizing, Named Layout Bounds, and Theme Tokenization

## Context
When the desktop shell moved the conversation (`#main`) to the center column and the Worker Companion inspector (`aside#detail`) to the right column, `body[data-workspace=split] .shell` in `desktop/ui/layout.css` kept the old column template (`var(--nav-width) var(--conversation-width) 16px minmax(560px, 1fr)`). That pinned the center conversation column to 420px (`360px..560px`) while the right companion panel absorbed `minmax(560px, 1fr)` (over half the window on widescreen viewports). In addition, layout breakpoints and pixel bounds were hardcoded in `Workspace.fit()` / `Workspace.update()`, and companion inspector cards in `desktop/ui/layout.css`, `desktop/ui/pages.css`, and `desktop/ui/app.js` used hardcoded dark-mode hex/rgba colors, a fallback `5 apps` count, and a `45%`/`68%` progress bar width.

## Decision
- Defined `Theme.LAYOUT_BOUNDS` in `desktop/ui/theme.js` with unit-suffixed constants (`navExpandedWidthPx`, `navCompactWidthPx`, `mobileMaxWidthPx`, `splitMinAvailablePx`, `autoOpenMinWidthPx`, `shortViewportMaxHeightPx`, `splitterWidthPx`, `splitterStepPx`, `splitterLargeStepPx`, `conversationMinWidthPx: 560`, `panelMinWidthPx: 300`, `panelDefaultWidthPx: 360`, `panelMaxWidthPx: 560`) and replaced `conversationWidthPx` with `panelWidthPx` (migrating stored `conversationWidthPx` records to `panelWidthPx: 360`).
- Updated `body[data-workspace=split] .shell` in `desktop/ui/layout.css` to `grid-template-columns: var(--nav-width) minmax(var(--conversation-min-width, 560px), 1fr) var(--splitter-width, 16px) var(--panel-width, 360px)` and updated `Workspace` in `desktop/ui/app.js` so `#main` is the flexible `1fr` primary column and `#detail` is a compact, resizable `300px..560px` (default `360px`) panel with natural left-to-widen / right-to-narrow splitter dragging and double-click reset.
- Replaced hardcoded hex/rgba colors across `.companion-*`, `.gbox*`, `.needs-*`, `.active-*`, `.sched-*`, `.tl-*`, `.appr-*`, `.papp-*`, and `.lib-*` in `desktop/ui/layout.css` and `desktop/ui/pages.css` with contrast-checked theme tokens.
- Connected every companion surface directly to the real server endpoints with no mocked fallbacks:
  - `refresh()` and `connectorsChanged()` in `desktop/ui/app.js` fetch `/v1/connectors` so `App.connectors` and `Lib.all` are populated from startup and `companionHeader()` always shows the real connected app count (including `0 apps`).
  - `task_json()` in `crates/magnus/src/api/work.rs` includes `plan` and `plan_version` on `/v1/tasks` list rows so `activityView()` reads the real kernel `Run.plan` units and connects the active card footer to `statusLineSlot()` (`desktop/ui/evidence.js`), omitting the progress bar when a task has no plan units instead of rendering a fake indeterminate bar.
  - `companionFooter()` returns `""` when no computer is provisioned (`!comp`) instead of rendering a fake sleeping bar, and wires `Wake VM →` (`data-wake-computer`) to `Server.computers.wake(comp.id)`.

## Reason
Restores the primary workspace space to the conversation column while keeping the Worker Companion panel compact and resizable, eliminates magic numbers and mocked UI values per the Magnus Code Standard, connects all companion widgets to live kernel endpoints, and ensures light and dark modes across all four themes preserve contrast invariants.
