# An invitation without email: a one-time password from the console (2026-11-07)

- When the provider cannot email an invitation (the realm has no mail server, or the mail does not arrive), the admin can make the invited person a one-time password in the console (`POST /api/v1/users/{id}/invite/password`, the "One-time password" button) and pass it on along with the provider's account page and the person's username. Reason: on strix-alpha the realm has no SMTP, and each invite ended at "the identity provider answered 500: ... Invalid sender address 'null'". The account had been made, but nobody could get in (#352). Test: `admin_users.rs` `without_email_an_admin_gives_an_invited_person_a_one_time_password`.
- The password is temporary at the provider (`reset-password` with `temporary: true`), so the person must still choose their own before they get a session, and they stay "invited" until they do. Reason: the admin never knows the password the person ends up using. Test: same; the one-time password alone gets no token, and the person is active only after choosing their own.
- Only for an invitation. An account in use is refused (`not_invited`), and an admin's invitation needs a fresh password, as inviting one does. Reason: otherwise the button would let an admin take over someone's account by resetting its password. Test: same.
- The console makes the password (20 letters and digits from the OS's randomness), shows it once and does not keep it. The audit log records `user_invite_password_set`, without the password. Reason: the admin passes it on, and the log must not hold a credential. Test: same (audit entry).
- Setting up the realm's mail server is not part of this change. On strix-alpha it is configured on the realm from files the owner writes. Reason: the console has to work without mail either way.
