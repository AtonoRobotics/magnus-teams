# Watched ids are deletable content (#408)

Codex P1 on #407: `WatchedSeen.ids` landed in the log body outside
the content store, so erasure could not remove watched item ids.

`watched_seen.ids` is registered in `events::CONTENT` with a
deserialization default, routing ids into the deletable store
through the generic split/join path. The log's backfill migration
re-splits old events against current CONTENT, so events logged
before this fix heal on rewrite.
