# One authorised read: destination previews, sent searches and polls pass one gate

**Choice.** The reads the server makes for a person without the model asking for them (a send's destination preview, #184; the sent search behind "mailed before", #210 and #371; a watched read's poll, #406) all go through `Kernel::authorised_read` (`authorised_read.rs`). It owns, in order: the read is a tool the app classes, and classes as a read; no hold stops the person's work on that app (`halts::may_proceed` at `Phase::Call`: the server's switch, the person's, their agent's, the app's, and the person turned off); the person may use the app; its live definition is the screened one (`check_pin`, now always with the run's id when there is a run); policy's decision; inside a run, that decision audited as a Decision of its own; and only then the call through the person's own sign-in. The callers (`gate_reads.rs` for the two gate reads, `watch_reads::poll_read`) keep only what they do with the answer and how they word a refusal.

**What changed for polls.** `poll_read` was a drifted copy: it had no policy decision and no hold. A poll is work done for its person through their sign-in, so per `2026-11-11-kill-switch-levels.md` ("A connector's switch … refuses any call to it at the boundary"; a person's switch covers "everything done for that person"; "Stopping is always safe, so the wider reading is the safer one") it is now refused under the server's switch, the person's, their agent's and the app's. `poll_due` skips those people and apps before marking a poll, and the read refuses again in case a switch was pulled since. A pause does not hold a poll: a pause stops new tasks and lets what runs go on, and a poll starts no task. A poll is judged by policy under the person's own layers, untainted, with no task layer (it has no task). A poll that finds the person not connected now refuses instead of calling the app with no sign-in.

**Polls are not audited as Decision events.** A Decision belongs to a run, and a poll is the kernel's own read with no run (`2026-11-11-polls-read-directly.md`). A refused poll is logged by `start_poll` as before; an allowed one leaves its `watch_polled` mark. Writing a Decision per poll would add an event per watch per interval for every person.

**A side read must be classed a read.** The gate reads were not checked for this before; a destination or sent read classed as a write is refused rather than made outside the model's view.
