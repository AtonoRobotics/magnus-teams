# Removing a commitment writes both events at once (2026-11-06)

- `remove_commitment` writes `removed` (the seqs of the commitment's events) and `commitment_removed` in one `emit_all`, `removed` first, then deletes the content. Before, it wrote `commitment_removed` alone and then `removed`: a stop between them left the commitment gone from the state, its words in the content store and backups, and no `removed` event for `Log::finish_removals` to finish at the next start, while a second DELETE answered 404. Now a stop after the write leaves a `removed` event, which the next start finishes; a stop before it leaves the commitment as it was, and the person removes it again. Reason: Codex's review of #291; #293. Test: `commitments.rs` `a_removal_is_recorded_before_the_commitment_leaves` (fails without the fix: the commitment left before its removal was recorded).
