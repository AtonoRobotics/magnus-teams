# A promise's date is the person's

**Choice.** A commitment due "by" a date (no time) falls due at the end of that date in its person's zone (`cadence::zone_of`, through `identity.zone`), not at midnight UTC. Every reader goes through `commitments::due` with the zone: the overdue mark in the app's list and in a run's commitments, their order, and the review's wake for a commitment coming due and its context. A day whose midnight the clocks skip ends at the first moment after the skip. A date with a time is read as given.

**Reason.** A person in Los Angeles who promised something "by Friday" saw it overdue at 4 p.m. on Friday, and the review woke for it on UTC's day. A date a person writes is a date on their own clock, as their schedules and quiet hours already are.
